现任职于太原理工大学，担任讲师。2026 年 3 月在东南大学获得博士学位，师从刘波教授，期间曾与紫金山实验室开展项目合作研究。2021 年在太原理工大学获得硕士学位，师从王莉教授。主要从事时空图数据挖掘研究及其在动态网络链接预测、网络异常行为分析、未知网络威胁检测与防御等领域的应用。曾参与国家重点研发计划项目和多项国家自然科学基金项目，在国内和国际著名学术期刊和会议上发表论文近 10 篇，曾受多个 CCF-A 类期刊和中科院 1 区期刊邀请担任匿名稿件评阅人。 Xiaorong Hao is currently a Lecturer at Taiyuan University of Technology. She received her Ph.D. degree from Southeast University in March 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Bo Liu, during which she collaborated on research projects with Purple Mountain Laboratories. She obtained her M.S. degree from Taiyuan University of Technology in 2021, advised by Prof. Li Wang. Her research mainly focuses on spatio-temporal graph data mining and its applications in dynamic network link prediction, network abnormal behavior analysis, unknown network threat detection and defense. She has participated in National Key R&D Program projects and multiple National Natural Science Foundation of China projects. She has published nearly 10 papers in prestigious domestic and international academic journals and conferences, and has been invited to serve as an anonymous reviewer for several CCF-A journals and CAS Tier 1 journals.
